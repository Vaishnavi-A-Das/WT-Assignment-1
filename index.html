About the Network

Established in 1979, Chaitanya Bharathi Institute of Technology, Gandipet, is one of the early private engineering colleges in India. Over the years we have given to society a huge number of developers, actors, politicians, and civil servants. Now it's time to connect to our roots, trace their journeys, and grow together as a community.
